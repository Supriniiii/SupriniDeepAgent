# Workflow Stages

## Stage 1 — Research Intake

**Purpose:** Understand the research objective and define the research question.

**Outputs:** Research question, scope definition, success criteria.

---

## Stage 2 — Research Planning

**Purpose:** Develop a structured research strategy.

**Responsible Component:** Research Planner

**Outputs:** Research plan, search strategies, source priorities.

---

## Stage 3 — Source Discovery

**Purpose:** Identify and prioritize information sources.

**Responsible Component:** Source Manager

**Outputs:** Source list, credibility assessments, access priorities.

---

## Stage 4 — Information Retrieval

**Purpose:** Gather information from identified sources.

**Responsible Component:** Executor

**Outputs:** Raw information, retrieved documents, search results.

---

## Stage 5 — Evidence Validation

**Purpose:** Assess credibility, relevance, and consistency of collected information.

**Responsible Component:** Evidence Manager + Verifier

**Outputs:** Validated evidence, rejected sources, credibility report.

---

## Stage 6 — Knowledge Synthesis

**Purpose:** Combine validated evidence into coherent findings.

**Responsible Component:** Synthesis Engine

**Outputs:** Synthesized findings, comparative analysis, conclusions.

---

## Stage 7 — Reflection

**Purpose:** Assess research completeness and identify gaps.

**Responsible Component:** Reflection

**Outputs:** Coverage assessment, gap analysis, refinement recommendations.

---

## Stage 8 — Report Generation

**Purpose:** Produce structured research deliverables.

**Outputs:** Research report, citations, executive summary.

---

## Stage 9 — Memory Update

**Purpose:** Persist research knowledge for future use.

**Responsible Component:** Memory Manager

**Outputs:** Updated research memory, source catalog.

# Decision Points

| Decision | Rule | Action |
|----------|------|--------|
| Insufficient evidence | Coverage below threshold | Return to Information Retrieval |
| Source unreliable | Credibility check fails | Exclude source, find alternative |
| Research incomplete | Reflection identifies gaps | Refine search strategy |
