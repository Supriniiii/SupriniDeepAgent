# Purpose

The Consistency Verifier checks generated outputs for internal contradictions, conflicting statements, and logical inconsistencies. It ensures that different parts of an output do not disagree with each other, that naming and terminology are used consistently, and that the overall output presents a coherent, non-contradictory whole. It catches the class of errors where individual parts are correct in isolation but conflict when combined.

---

# Responsibilities

- Scan outputs for conflicting statements or contradictory claims
- Verify consistent use of terminology, naming, and conventions throughout
- Check cross-references and internal links for accuracy
- Detect logical contradictions between different sections or artifacts
- Verify numerical consistency (totals match details, dates align)
- Check that interface definitions match their implementations
- Verify that dependencies declared in one place are acknowledged in others
- Report inconsistencies with specific locations and conflict details

---

# Scope

This component covers internal consistency verification. It detects contradictions WITHIN an output or between related outputs generated together. It does NOT verify correctness against external requirements (that is the Verifier's job), check policy compliance (that is the Policy Verifier's job), or assess quality (that is the Evaluator's job). It answers: "Does this output agree with itself?"

# State Management

The Consistency Verifier is stateless across invocations. Each consistency check operates independently on the provided artifacts. During a single check, it builds a transient map of claims, references, and term usage that is discarded after findings are emitted.

---

# Error Handling

- Artifact too large for full scan: Apply sampling strategy, note partial coverage in findings
- Terminology glossary unavailable: Skip terminology checks, flag in report
- Logic checker timeout: Report partial results, flag unchecked sections
- Ambiguous contradiction: Report as potential inconsistency with low confidence
- Unparseable artifact section: Skip section, report as unchecked area

# Validation Requirements

- All output sections must be checked (no silent skips without reporting)
- Contradictions must cite both conflicting locations with exact text
- Consistency score must reflect the severity-weighted finding count
- Terminology violations must reference the expected standard term
- Cross-reference checks must verify bidirectional accuracy
