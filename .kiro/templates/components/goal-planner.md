# Purpose

The Goal Planner defines, refines, and prioritizes engineering goals derived from business objectives. It translates high-level intent into measurable, actionable goals with clear success criteria, then ranks them based on value, urgency, dependencies, and resource availability. It ensures that downstream planning operates against well-defined targets rather than ambiguous requests.

---

# Responsibilities

- Receive raw business objectives and user intent
- Refine ambiguous objectives into precise, measurable goals
- Define success criteria and key results for each goal
- Prioritize goals based on business value, urgency, and dependencies
- Detect conflicting goals and resolve or escalate
- Decompose compound goals into independent sub-goals
- Maintain goal hierarchy and traceability to original objectives
- Emit prioritized goal sets for downstream planning components

---

# Scope

This component covers goal definition, refinement, and prioritization. It transforms "what the user wants" into "what must be achieved" with precision. It does NOT plan how to achieve goals (that is the Planner's job) or select strategies (that is the Strategy Planner's job). It operates at the intent-to-goal boundary.

# State Management

The Goal Planner maintains a transient goal graph during refinement and prioritization. Once the prioritized goal set is emitted, internal state is discarded. Goal persistence is the responsibility of the downstream goal registry. For iterative refinement sessions, prior goal sets are passed back as input.

---

# Error Handling

- Ambiguous objectives: Request clarification from the user via orchestrator
- Conflicting goals: Attempt resolution using priority rules; escalate if unresolvable
- Unmeasurable goals: Flag as incomplete and require success criteria before proceeding
- Resource-impossible goals: Mark as infeasible with explanation
- Priority deadlock: Apply default ordering rules and flag for human review

# Validation Requirements

- Every goal must have at least one measurable success criterion
- Goal hierarchy must be acyclic
- No duplicate goals within the same objective context
- Priority scores must be justified by at least one criterion
- All goals must trace back to the originating business objective
