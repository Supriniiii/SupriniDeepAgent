# Purpose

Provides persistent storage and retrieval of engineering knowledge, organizational standards, learned patterns, and historical information that persists across multiple sessions and supports continuous improvement.

---

# Responsibilities

- Store persistent knowledge.
- Retrieve historical information.
- Manage knowledge versioning.
- Apply retention policies.
- Support semantic search.
- Maintain knowledge consistency.
- Archive obsolete information.
- Support knowledge evolution.

---

# Scope

Covers persistent cross-session knowledge storage. Does not manage working memory, session state, or context assembly.
