# Purpose

This document captures significant design decisions, the context in which they were made, the alternatives considered, and the rationale for the chosen approach. It provides a decision log that prevents repeated debates, preserves institutional knowledge, and enables future teams to understand why the system is built the way it is.

---

# Scope

This document covers architectural and design decisions with system-wide or module-wide impact, including technology selections, pattern choices, trade-off resolutions, and constraint accommodations. It does not cover implementation-level choices, coding style preferences, or operational procedures.

---

# Audience

- Architects reviewing or evolving the system design
- Senior engineers onboarding to the project
- Technical leads assessing decision currency and validity
- Future teams evaluating whether to revisit past decisions

---

# Prerequisites

- Understanding of the system's business requirements and constraints
- Familiarity with the architectural patterns under consideration
- Knowledge of the technology landscape and available alternatives
- Context on organizational constraints (budget, timeline, skills)
