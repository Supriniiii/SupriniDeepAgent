# Purpose

This document defines the disaster recovery strategy, procedures, and recovery objectives for restoring system operations after a catastrophic failure. It ensures the organization can recover from data loss, infrastructure failures, or regional outages within defined time and data loss tolerances.

---

# Scope

This document covers recovery point objectives (RPO), recovery time objectives (RTO), backup strategies, failover procedures, data restoration, and disaster recovery testing. It does not cover routine maintenance, minor incident response, or application-level bug fixes.

---

# Audience

- SRE teams responsible for system resilience
- Infrastructure engineers managing backup and replication
- Business continuity planners defining recovery objectives
- Executive stakeholders approving disaster recovery investments

---

# Prerequisites

- Defined RPO and RTO targets approved by business stakeholders
- Access to backup systems and disaster recovery infrastructure
- Understanding of data replication and consistency models
- Knowledge of geographic redundancy and failover mechanisms
