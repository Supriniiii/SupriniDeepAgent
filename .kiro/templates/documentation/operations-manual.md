# Purpose

This document provides operational runbooks, monitoring procedures, incident response protocols, and routine maintenance tasks for keeping the system running in production. It ensures on-call engineers and operations staff can respond to issues, perform maintenance, and maintain system health without requiring developer intervention.

---

# Scope

This document covers operational runbooks, monitoring and alerting configuration, incident response procedures, routine maintenance tasks, and escalation paths. It does not cover feature development, architecture design, or initial deployment setup.

---

# Audience

- On-call engineers responding to production incidents
- SRE teams maintaining system reliability
- Operations staff performing routine maintenance
- Incident managers coordinating response efforts

---

# Prerequisites

- Access to production monitoring dashboards and alerting systems
- Understanding of the system's architecture and critical paths
- Familiarity with the incident management process and tools
- Access to production infrastructure with appropriate permissions
