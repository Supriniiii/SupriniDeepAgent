# Purpose

This document captures the complete set of functional and non-functional requirements for the system, including business goals, user stories, acceptance criteria, constraints, and traceability to architectural decisions. It serves as the single source of truth for what the system must deliver.

---

# Scope

This document covers business objectives, functional requirements, non-functional requirements (performance, security, availability), constraints, assumptions, dependencies, and requirement traceability. It does not cover implementation design, technology selection, or deployment procedures.

---

# Audience

- Product managers defining and prioritizing capabilities
- Architects deriving design decisions from requirements
- Engineers implementing against acceptance criteria
- QA teams deriving test cases from requirements

---

# Prerequisites

- Business case or project charter approved by stakeholders
- Stakeholder interviews or discovery sessions completed
- Understanding of the problem domain and user personas
- Access to related system documentation and constraints
