# Purpose

The Enterprise Harness provides a production-grade execution environment for Deep Agents operating within corporate governance, compliance, and operational frameworks. It enforces enterprise policies, integrates with corporate identity providers, respects organizational hierarchies, and maintains full audit trails to satisfy regulatory requirements while enabling AI-driven automation at enterprise scale.

---

# Runtime Overview

This harness operates within enterprise security boundaries with integration to corporate SSO, RBAC systems, compliance engines, and change management workflows. It enforces data classification policies, maintains segregation of duties, supports multi-tenancy with organizational isolation, and provides the governance controls required for deployment in regulated industries including finance, healthcare, and government.

# Error Handling

The Enterprise Harness classifies errors by business impact severity and routes them through the corporate incident management system. Compliance-relevant failures trigger immediate audit events and may halt processing pending human review. Data handling errors that could affect regulatory standing are escalated through the governance chain with full context preservation for post-incident analysis and regulatory reporting.

# Validation Requirements

- All agent actions must be attributable to authenticated identities through the corporate identity provider
- Data access must respect classification levels and need-to-know policies enforced by the authorization layer
- Audit trails must be tamper-evident and retained for the period required by the applicable compliance framework
- Change management approvals must be obtained before agents modify production systems or controlled data
- Multi-tenancy boundaries must prevent cross-tenant data leakage under all failure conditions
