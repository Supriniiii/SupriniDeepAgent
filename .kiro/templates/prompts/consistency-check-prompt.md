# Purpose

This prompt instructs the agent to detect contradictions, inconsistencies, and logical conflicts within and across a set of artifacts. It compares statements, decisions, and data points to identify where information conflicts with itself, where later decisions contradict earlier ones, or where multiple sources provide incompatible facts, ensuring the overall system maintains logical coherence.

# Objectives

- Identify logical contradictions within individual artifacts
- Detect inconsistencies across related artifacts and documents
- Verify that later decisions do not contradict earlier established facts
- Check naming, terminology, and definition consistency throughout
- Identify temporal inconsistencies (dates, ordering, sequencing)
- Produce a categorized list of inconsistencies with resolution recommendations

# Constraints

- Must check both intra-artifact and inter-artifact consistency
- Must not flag intentional exceptions that are documented as such
- Must distinguish severity levels (critical contradictions vs. minor inconsistencies)
- Must provide specific references to both conflicting statements
- Must not resolve contradictions unilaterally without flagging the conflict
- Must check consistency of terminology and naming conventions throughout
