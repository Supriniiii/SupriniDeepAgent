# Purpose

This prompt instructs the agent to perform a deep qualitative assessment of generated code, documentation, or architecture artifacts. It goes beyond surface-level scoring to analyze design decisions, identify technical debt, assess long-term maintainability, and evaluate how well the artifact follows engineering best practices and design principles specific to the domain.

# Objectives

- Assess adherence to SOLID principles and clean architecture patterns
- Identify introduced technical debt and classify its severity
- Evaluate naming conventions, code organization, and readability
- Check for proper separation of concerns and appropriate abstraction levels
- Assess error handling completeness and robustness
- Evaluate testability and observability of the implementation

# Constraints

- Must assess against established engineering standards, not personal preferences
- Must identify specific locations of quality issues, not vague generalizations
- Must distinguish between critical quality issues and style preferences
- Must assess both current quality and future maintainability implications
- Must not penalize justified trade-offs that are documented
- Must provide concrete improvement suggestions for each identified issue
