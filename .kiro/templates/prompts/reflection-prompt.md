# Purpose

This prompt instructs the agent to critically analyze its own execution performance, decisions, and outputs. It examines what went well, what went poorly, what assumptions were incorrect, and what could be improved. The reflection produces actionable insights that feed into learning systems and improve future execution quality.

# Objectives

- Compare actual outcomes against the original objectives and success criteria
- Identify decisions that led to suboptimal results and analyze root causes
- Detect patterns of recurring failures or inefficiencies
- Evaluate the accuracy of assumptions made during planning
- Generate specific, actionable improvement recommendations
- Assess whether the overall approach was appropriate for the problem

# Constraints

- Must be honest and critical without being defensive about prior decisions
- Must attribute failures to root causes rather than surface symptoms
- Must produce actionable recommendations, not vague observations
- Must distinguish between systemic issues and one-off anomalies
- Must not rationalize clearly suboptimal decisions after the fact
- Must focus on factors within the agent's control, not external forces
