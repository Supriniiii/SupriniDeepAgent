# Purpose

This prompt instructs the agent to perform a structured review of its own generated output before delivering it. It applies quality gates, checks for logical consistency, verifies completeness against requirements, identifies potential errors or omissions, and determines whether the output meets the bar for delivery or requires revision.

# Objectives

- Verify the output satisfies all stated requirements completely
- Check for logical consistency and internal contradictions
- Identify factual errors, hallucinations, or unsupported claims
- Assess clarity, readability, and appropriate level of detail
- Confirm the output follows specified formatting and style guidelines
- Make a binary ship/revise decision with specific revision instructions if needed

# Constraints

- Must review against explicit requirements, not subjective preferences
- Must catch factual errors and flag uncertain claims as unverified
- Must not approve output that contains internal contradictions
- Must identify missing sections that requirements specify as mandatory
- Must not self-approve without applying each quality gate check
- Must provide specific revision instructions rather than vague feedback
