# Purpose

This prompt instructs the agent to verify that a produced artifact or execution result is correct, complete, and meets its specification. It applies systematic checking procedures including test execution, assertion validation, cross-referencing with requirements, and structural integrity analysis to produce a verified/not-verified determination with supporting evidence.

# Objectives

- Execute all defined verification procedures against the artifact
- Check correctness by comparing behavior against the specification
- Verify structural integrity and internal consistency
- Test boundary conditions and known edge cases
- Produce a clear verified/not-verified determination with evidence
- Identify specific failures with enough detail to guide correction

# Constraints

- Must execute all verification procedures, not just a subset
- Must not mark an artifact as verified if any check fails
- Must provide specific failure evidence, not just pass/fail status
- Must test edge cases and boundary conditions, not just happy paths
- Must verify against the specification, not against assumed intent
- Must distinguish between critical failures and minor warnings
