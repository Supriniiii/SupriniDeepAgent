# Purpose

This report provides a high-level executive summary of overall system quality, synthesizing data from test results, coverage metrics, defect analysis, performance benchmarks, and security assessments into a single quality score and readiness determination. It enables leadership to make informed go/no-go decisions based on quantitative quality evidence.

---

# Scope

Covers: Composite quality score calculation, dimensional quality breakdown (functional, performance, security, reliability), quality gate pass/fail determination, risk assessment summary, quality trend over releases, comparison against quality targets, and actionable improvement recommendations.

Excludes: Detailed test case results, individual defect descriptions, raw performance data, and technical implementation specifics.

---

# Test Objectives

- Calculate composite quality score from weighted dimensional assessments
- Determine quality gate pass/fail status against configured thresholds
- Summarize risk exposure based on open defects, coverage gaps, and performance issues
- Track quality trend across recent releases to assess trajectory
- Identify top quality improvement opportunities ranked by impact
- Deliver clear go/no-go recommendation with supporting evidence
