# Purpose

This test validates the reflection capability of an agent, verifying that it can evaluate its own outputs for quality, identify errors or gaps in its reasoning, self-correct before producing final results, and improve its approach iteratively. It ensures the reflection loop produces measurably better outputs compared to single-pass execution.

---

# Scope

Covers: Self-evaluation of output quality against defined criteria, error detection in prior reasoning steps, self-correction logic and re-generation, iterative improvement through multiple reflection passes, reflection termination conditions (convergence or max iterations), and quality score comparison between pre-reflection and post-reflection outputs.

Excludes: External feedback loops from humans or other agents, long-term learning across sessions, memory-based improvement over time, and LLM model quality evaluation.

---

# Test Objectives

- Verify the agent detects quality issues in its initial output through self-evaluation
- Confirm self-correction produces measurably improved output on identified issues
- Validate that reflection terminates after convergence or maximum iteration limit
- Ensure reflection does not degrade already-correct portions of the output
- Verify reflection criteria align with documented quality standards
- Confirm reflection metadata (iterations, scores, corrections) is logged for observability

# Test Procedure

```text
1. Submit a task known to produce imperfect results on first pass
2. Capture the agent's initial output before reflection
3. Trigger the reflection loop and observe self-evaluation execution
4. Verify the agent identifies specific quality issues in its initial output
5. Capture the corrected output after reflection and compare to initial
6. Measure quality improvement using defined evaluation metrics
7. Test convergence by running reflection on already-high-quality output
8. Verify maximum iteration limit terminates reflection that does not converge
9. Confirm reflection does not degrade correct portions of the output
10. Verify reflection metadata (iteration count, scores, corrections) is logged
```

# Validation Rules

- Post-reflection output quality score must be equal to or higher than pre-reflection score
- Reflection must identify at least one actionable issue when quality is below threshold
- Maximum iteration limit must always halt reflection regardless of convergence state
- Correct portions of output must not regress between reflection iterations
- Reflection metadata must capture iteration count, quality scores, and correction descriptions
