# Purpose

Calculates weighted quality scores across all engineering dimensions to produce a comprehensive engineering health assessment, enabling objective comparison and continuous improvement tracking.

---

# Validation Objectives

- Calculate engineering quality scores.
- Assess each engineering dimension.
- Produce weighted overall score.
- Compare against quality thresholds.
- Track improvement trends.
- Generate actionable recommendations.

```yaml

    condition: Overall score above minimum threshold
```
