# Purpose

Verifies that the generated solution satisfies all functional and non-functional requirements, acceptance criteria are met, constraints are respected, and requirement traceability is complete.

---

# Validation Objectives

- Verify functional requirement coverage.
- Verify non-functional requirement coverage.
- Confirm acceptance criteria satisfaction.
- Validate constraint compliance.
- Ensure requirement traceability.
- Detect missing requirements.

- Every requirement has a unique identifier.
- Every requirement is traceable to implementation.
- Acceptance criteria are testable.
- Constraints are documented and validated.
- No orphaned requirements exist.
