# Purpose

Maps every requirement to its architectural implementation, component realization, test coverage, and validation evidence, providing complete engineering traceability from business need to delivered solution.

---

# Validation Objectives

- Map requirements to architecture.
- Map architecture to components.
- Map components to tests.
- Map tests to validation evidence.
- Identify traceability gaps.
- Ensure complete coverage.
